About

Most tech leaders I talk to don’t have a technology problem. They have a capacity problem. At Singula Team, we build remote engineering teams that feel like an extension of yours — shipping web and mobile products, moving systems to the cloud, and turning legacy stacks into lean architectures.

I started as a hands-on engineer — Ruby on Rails, React, AWS — including time at Babbel scaling infrastructure that served millions of requests a day. Mentoring developers at mkdev and years in business development at Evrone taught me how products get built and how partnerships actually work. Today I’m still a product engineer at heart — just building teams instead of features, as founder and CEO of Singula Team. And I still build things myself: when a client needs a quick prototype I can ship it hands-on before the team scales it.
